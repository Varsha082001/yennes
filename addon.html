Our Tally Add-ons

Practical tools for everyday business needs.

01

Auto Backup in Tally

Automatically backs up company data in the background to help safeguard important business information without interrupting your work.

02

Alert System

Supports email alerts when vouchers are saved and emails related to outstanding balances, helping teams stay informed.

03

Address Book

View ledger details at a glance, including name, address, contact number, mobile, email, state, GSTIN and credit limit.

04

Ledger & Item Deactivation

Hide inactive ledgers and item masters from new voucher entries while retaining their visibility in reports and previous vouchers.

05

Voucher Approval

Assign authorisation rights for selected voucher types. Entries can follow an approval workflow before they are authorised.

06

Invoice Management

Customise invoice and report printing with options such as gridlines, previous balance, stock-group-wise invoices and user details.

07

Document Management System

Attach relevant documents to vouchers, ledgers and stock items, and access them from supported Tally screens and reports.

08

Gridlines in Tally

Improve readability by adding horizontal gridlines to supported Tally reports and invoice printouts. Compatibility depends on the version and setup.

09

Negative Stock Monitoring

Helps identify negative inventory situations so your team can investigate stock discrepancies and improve inventory control.

10

Recycle Bin in Tally

Provides a recovery workflow for supported deleted records, helping users retrieve accidentally removed information where available.

11

Control System Management

Support business control processes with tailored Tally workflows and settings based on your organisation's requirements.

12

Digital Signature in Tally

Supports digital signing and authentication of documents through a suitable digital-signature integration and configuration.
