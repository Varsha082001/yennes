WHO WE ARE

Delivering Innovative Tally Solutions for Businesses Across Industries

YENNES Infotec (P) Limited with its Head Office (HO) at Chennai and Branches at Coimbatore, Madurai, Puducherry, Salem, Tirunelveli, Trichy and Tirupur is a Government and Very Large Accounts (GVLA) 5 star certified Sales & Solution Partner of Tally, India’s leading business management software company. YENNES has expertise around all Tally products and has been selling, implementing, customizing and supporting customers all over India for more than 3 decades.

"Our mission is to empower businesses with innovative technology solutions that simplify operations and drive digital transformation."

Mr. N S Saravanan

Director & CEO
How We Started

Founded in Year 1995

YENNES Infotec (P) Limited began its journey with a vision to empower businesses through reliable and innovative accounting solutions. Established over three decades ago, the company started as a trusted technology partner dedicated to helping organizations simplify their financial and business management processes.

As the demand for Tally solutions grew, YENNES expanded its expertise in software implementation, customization, training, and customer support. With a strong commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, the company steadily built long-term relationships with businesses across various industries.

Today, YENNES has grown into a Government and Very Large Accounts (GVLA) 5-Star Certified Sales & Solution Partner of Tally, serving customers through its Head Office in Chennai and branches in Coimbatore, Madurai, Puducherry, Salem, Tirunelveli, Trichy, and Tirupur. Backed by more than 30 years of experience, YENNES continues to deliver trusted Tally solutions and exceptional customer service across India.

Our Vision

The vision of YENNES can be summarised with 4Ps.

  • People: Being the “Go to” workplace where people are inspired to be the best they can be.
  • Portfolio: Offering our customers a portfolio of products and services that anticipate and satisfy their current and future requirements.
  • Partners: Being the partner of choice by nurturing a network of partners built on mutual loyalty.
  • Profit: Maximizing return to all stakeholders while being mindful of our overall responsibilities.
