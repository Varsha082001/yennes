WHO WE ARE

Delivering Innovative Tally Solutions Across Industries

YENNES Infotec (P) Limited with its Head Office (HO) at Chennai and Branches at Coimbatore, Madurai, Puducherry, Salem, Tirunelveli, Trichy and Tirupur is a Government and Very Large Accounts (GVLA) 5 star certified Sales & Solution Partner of Tally, India’s leading business management software company. YENNES has expertise around all Tally products and has been selling, implementing, customizing and supporting customers all over India for more than 3 decades.

As a GVLA and a 5 Star certified Solutions and Services Partner of Tally, YENNES is best placed to help customers design, implement, integrate and support Tally while providing business specific customization. The collaboration between Tally and YENNES ensures the customer gets the best out of Tally.

Being a long standing partner, the relationship with Tally ensures YENNES access to a much broader range of software tools and solutions for its clients. YENNES, by leveraging on this is able to innovate on the Tally platform developing new skills and capabilities. This armed with in depth knowledge of Tally helps YENNES add that extra bit in value to customers.

While YENNES delights the customer with its domain and technological expertise, the belief is that the real value created to the customer always lies in the relationship. The contracts, while having an eye for detail are also flexible enough to ensure a relationship beyond the contract, as future is always a changing constant. These values and beliefs are deeply ingrained in the YENNES team that they are empowered to bring in a relationship rather than just a contract.

OUR JOURNEY

Three Decades of Trust & Excellence

Since 1995, YENNES has built a strong reputation through reliable Tally solutions, continuous innovation, and a commitment to customer satisfaction.

1995

Where Our Story Began

YENNES began its journey with a vision to help businesses manage their accounting and business processes more efficiently through reliable technology solutions.

Growth & Innovation

Expanding Our Expertise

Over the years, YENNES expanded its expertise in Tally software implementation, TDL customization, training, and customer support to meet the evolving needs of businesses across various industries.

Today

A Trusted Tally Partner

Today, YENNES is a Government and Very Large Accounts (GVLA) 5-Star Certified Sales & Solution Partner of Tally, serving customers through its head office in Chennai and branches in Coimbatore, Madurai, Puducherry, Salem, Tirunelveli, Trichy, and Tirupur. With over 30 years of experience, YENNES continues to deliver dependable Tally solutions and dedicated customer support across Tamil Nadu and Puducherry.
